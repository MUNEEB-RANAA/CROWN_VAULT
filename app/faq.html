CrownVault FAQ

Common questions.

Does CrownVault process real payments?

No. This build creates local demo orders so the complete marketplace flow can be tested without a live payment gateway.

Are watches professionally authenticated?

Not in this local implementation. Listing states and product information are part of the marketplace demo and should not be presented as professional authentication.

What happens when I submit a watch for sale?

The backend stores the listing as Pending Review and it appears in the signed-in user's dashboard.

Can I compare watches?

Yes. Add up to three watches from product cards or detail pages and open the Compare page.
